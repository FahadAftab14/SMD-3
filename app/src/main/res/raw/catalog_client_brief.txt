CLIENT BRIEF - SERVICE PAGE WEBSITE

Goal
Create a page that explains the services our company offers. The page must feel clean, modern and trustworthy.

Look and feel
- Main colors: blue and red, with plenty of white space.
- Friendly, simple language. No technical jargon.
- Every service gets its own product card.

Each product card needs
1. A short title.
2. Two lines of description.
3. A starting price.
4. A "Learn more" button.

Pages and sections
- Hero banner with a headline and a call to action.
- Services grid (six cards).
- Pricing table that works on small screens.
- Contact form with validation.

Deadline
First low fidelity version in one week. Final design approved by the client before development starts.
