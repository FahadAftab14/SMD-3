BRAND GUIDELINES

Logo
Use the full-color logo on white or very light backgrounds. Keep clear space equal to the height of the letter "S" on every side.

Colors
Primary blue   #2F9BF4
Deep navy      #1A1A2E
Accent purple  #7B5CE0
Soft pink      #FF6FA5

Typography
Headings: bold, sentence case.
Body text: regular, 14 to 16 px, high contrast on light backgrounds.

Do
- Keep layouts simple and aligned to an 8 px grid.
- Use one accent color per screen.

Do not
- Stretch or recolor the logo.
- Put body text on busy images.
